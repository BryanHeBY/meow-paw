Meow Paw Windows portable bundle: baseline third-party notices
==============================================================

This is a partial inventory, not a release audit. It does not identify every
dependency or data set linked into the two executables. It does not authorize
redistribution of the baseline ZIP. The separate Paw and Meow notices come
from LICENSE at the exact Paw commit and pinned Meow gitlink in manifest.json.

Verified notice sources
-----------------------

- GPUI Kit 0.11.0 (Apache-2.0): gpui-base and gpui-component published crate
  LICENSE-APACHE are identical. The published gpui-kit and gpui-kit-assets
  manifests also identify Apache-2.0.
  https://github.com/longbridge/gpui-kit
- GPUI pre 0.3.14 (Apache-2.0): the gpui-pre published crate LICENSE-APACHE
  includes the Zed Industries copyright. The inspected 0.3.14 companion
  crate copies were identical.
  https://github.com/zed-industries/zed
- Icons from gpui-kit-assets 0.11.0: the complete published LICENSE-LUCIDE
  includes both the Lucide ISC notice and the Feather-derived icons' MIT
  notice and attribution.
  https://crates.io/crates/gpui-kit-assets/0.11.0
- option-ext 0.2.0 (MPL-2.0) appears in the locked x86_64-pc-windows-msvc
  Paw desktop normal dependency graph. The published source snapshot is:
  https://static.crates.io/crates/option-ext/option-ext-0.2.0.crate
  Cargo.lock archive SHA-256:
  04744f49eae99ab78e0d5c0b603ab218f515ea8cfe5a456d7629ad883a3b6e7d
  Check that the covered source in the actual executable, including any
  modifications, is made available before distribution under MPL section 3.2.
- webpki-roots 1.0.11 (CDLA-Permissive-2.0) also appears in that graph.
  Its published LICENSE accompanies the certificate data notice.
  https://crates.io/crates/webpki-roots/1.0.11
- WireGuard LLC Wintun 0.14.1: the pinned default/full Windows Meow build can
  embed the signed prebuilt DLL even when there is no top-level wintun.dll.
  This is the Prebuilt Binaries License copied verbatim from wintun/LICENSE.txt
  in the official archive. It is not the GPL-2.0 license for Wintun source.
  https://www.wintun.net/builds/wintun-0.14.1.zip
  Official archive SHA-256:
  07c256185d6ee3652e09fa55c0b673e2624b565e02c4b9091c79ca7d2f24ef51
  The embedded-only packaging layout has not been confirmed under the
  prebuilt terms governing distribution alongside software using the
  permitted API. Including the text does not resolve that question.
